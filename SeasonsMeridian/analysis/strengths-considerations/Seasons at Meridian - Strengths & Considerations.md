**A3. ### Bottom line

The strengths cluster into a coherent thesis: **a completed lease-up bought at replacement-cost parity, ~11% per unit inside the only same-vintage comp, with live leasing evidence at or above the underwriting, into a 2027-29 supply vacuum that is defended by financing math, entitlement politics, and land scarcity simultaneously.** The considerations cluster into four honest counter-theses: (1) the price needs the forward run-rate and a thin exit cap in the market's least-liquid product type, against rates that have already moved the wrong way; (2) the moat is a *financing* moat that reverses on a credit rally and does nothing for a demand shock in a metro whose job engine stalled in 2025 and has only just rebounded; (3) the revenue bridge (concession burn-off + LTL capture through a ~40%-retention turn machine, into a winter the asset has never survived cleanly) is aggressive vs every third-party forecast; and (4) a cluster of unclosed diligence items — the two-showings discrepancy, the seller/lease-term/occupancy dressing questions, the subject's own DA, the PCA gap, and the live concession banners — is resolvable but not yet resolved.
