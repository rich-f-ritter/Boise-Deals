H38

Demand absorbed the biggest supply wave in ring history without breaking the subject — and same-store rents have now round-tripped to new highs. 3,694 units delivered across 27 communities 2022-2026 (962 / 1,003 / 1,441 / 48 / 240 by year); absorption has outrun supply for 8 straight quarters; 5-mi vacancy fell 15.2% → 5.4% (2024Q3 → 2026Q3). Rebuilt property-by-property from the CoStar 5-mi Property Analytics (inventory-weighted; same-store = the 86 properties / 10,518 units standing at 2022Q1, stripping new-delivery mix): same-store asking went $1,589 (2022Q1) → peak $1,619 (2022Q3) → trough $1,539 (2023Q4, −4.9%) → $1,654 at 2026Q3 — through the prior peak (+7.5% off trough, +5.4% YoY); effective cut deeper (−6.6% peak-to-trough, $1,590 → $1,485) and has recovered +9.9% to $1,632 (+5.8% YoY, +2.6% above its prior peak), with concessions 1.0% → 3.4% → 1.2%. The pre-wave stock absorbed ~3,000 competing new units and still made new rent highs. (Methodology: CoStar's 5-mi "Overall" series prints higher — $1,691 ask / $1,667 eff — because 2024-26 deliveries enter the average at higher rents, and the raw property grid even carries modeled rents for properties before they exist — the subject shows a $1,767 "asking rent" in 2022Q1 with zero units built; the same-store cut is the honest read of market movement.) The full MSA corroborates the shape and the ring leads it. On a triangulated best-estimate asking series (RealPage 2006-20, RealPage/CoStar average 2021-on — CoStar's pre-2018 backcast runs high vs RealPage $929 / Yardi $941 in 2016): metro asking peaked at $1,656 (2022Q3), troughed at $1,572 (2023Q4, −5.1%), and set a new all-time high of $1,716 in 2026Q2 (+9.2% off trough); every underlying vendor print is also at/above record (RealPage effective $1,727, CoStar asking $1,702), with RealPage showing the sharpest cycle (−9.5% effective drawdown) and Yardi's survey the mildest. The rent inflection maps one-for-one onto the delivery schedule: metro deliveries ran 2,157-3,422/yr through the 2018-24 wave (2023 peak), collapsed to 1,512 in 2025, and rents broke to new highs as the wave cleared. Long-run context: Boise asking rents compounded ~+6.5%/yr over 10 years and ~+5.0%/yr over 20 (2.6x since 2006) against ~2%+/yr population growth in every lookback window — the 2022-25 flat stretch is the supply wave, not a demand break.
