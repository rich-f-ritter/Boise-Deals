SUBJECT & ROAD CALLOUTS
Seasons at Meridian (subject)
2700 E Overland Rd · 360 units · 2024 · SE quadrant of Eagle Rd (SH-55) & I-84.
■ I-84 · 132-142K vehicles/day
COMPASS counts on the segments flanking the Eagle Rd interchange (~2023) — the metro's busiest freeway corridor. Subject sits at Exit 46; two-lane ramp upgrade designed (ITD, construction pending funding).
■ EAGLE RD (SH-55) · 60-65K vehicles/day
ITD: 'Idaho's busiest non-interstate highway' — ~60,000 vehicles/day, doubled since 2000; ACHD counted 64,000+ just north of I-84 and projects 78,000+ by 2040. The Eagle/Overland corner by the subject carries 76,500+ vehicles/day combined.
■ Eagle & Fairview — Idaho's busiest intersection
Ranked Idaho's #1 intersection by traffic count since 2005 (ACHD) — the retail gravity center the subject feeds into, 2 mi north.
EMPLOYMENT & MEDICAL
1 0.62 miEagle View Landing
Ahlquist's master-planned campus between Overland Rd and I-84 east of Eagle, anchoring the subject's quadrant: ICCU Building (125K SF, 2020), the POWER Engineers building, Topgolf, Hyatt Place and Holiday Inn Express, Office Building 2 tenants (GCU, Nightforce Optics, Great West Casualty, Ardurra), Norco HQ (~350 employees); St. Luke's campus adjoins to the north. Across the combined Eagle/Overland node (with Silverstone and El Dorado): 49 tenants ≥10K SF / 2.5M SF / ~4,900 reported jobs (CoStar); 22 office buildings / 914,000 SF built since 2020 within 1.25 mi of the subject.
