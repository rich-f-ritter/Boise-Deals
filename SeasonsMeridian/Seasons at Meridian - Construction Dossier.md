## 7. The 8' ceiling question — assessment

**The OM's Building Detail table says "Ceiling Height 8'." Surprisingly, this is probably accurate for
typical units — not a typo — but it must be verified because it cuts against the Class-A story.**

Evidence it's real:
1. The OM's own amenity page hedges: "High ceilings **\*available in select units**" — marketing would never
   footnote that if the standard unit were 9'.
2. BoiseDev's July 2024 walkthrough: "five penthouse units with **16-foot ceilings**" — called out as
   exceptional, implying the base product is not high-ceiling.
3. Matt Naumann (CBRE, 8/6 call): "a dozen, maybe 15 that have 15-foot ceilings the way the buildings lay
   out… rented for $2,600–2,700 vs $2,100 for the same floor plan" — again framed as the rare exception.
4. Marketing photos: upper cabinets run flush to the ceiling with no filler/soffit gap — a standard
   36" base + 18" splash + 42" upper stack tops out at exactly 96" AFF, i.e., an 8' plate. At 9' you'd
   see a ~12" reveal or crown.
5. The spec table reads like it was lifted from the actual drawings (SER feeder gauges, NFPA 13R edition,
   AcrylX, UV-Shield PEXa) — not boilerplate.

Why it still needs verification:
- 9' plates are the 2024 Class-A standard (The Aren, Dovetail vintage comps likely 9'). If truly 8',
  that is a genuine product deficiency vs. newer competition — relevant to our exit story, to the
  "like-quality replication rent" premium we quote ($2,120–$2,260), and to how hard we lean on the
  "premium product" narrative with IC.
- Possible nuance: some garden builders run 8' on floors 1–2 and 9'/vaulted on floor 3. The "way the
  buildings lay out" comment suggests the 15–16' units are top-floor vault conditions.

**Photogrammetric verification (8/17/2026):** measured from the OM's own photography, using
code-fixed dimensions as scale references.
- *Balcony-rail method* (OM p.20 exterior, 3-story balcony stack): the top rails on floors 2 and 3
  each sit exactly 42" above their decks, so rail-top-to-rail-top = floor-to-floor. Pixel spans:
  rail-3 = 72 px/42", rail-2 = 90 px/42" (perspective gradient), rail-to-rail = ~200 px. Integrating
  the projective scale across the span gives **floor-to-floor ≈ 108–109"** — i.e., **8'0" ceiling +
  ~12–13" floor assembly**. A 9' plate would require ~121–122", far outside the measurement band (±5").
- *Door method* (same photo, ground floor): the 6'8" entry door spans 206 px → 2.58 px/in; walkway to
  the entry-recess soffit measures ~233 px ≈ **90.5"** — consistent only with an ~8' ground-floor plate.
- *Interior photos*: upper cabinets run flush to the ceiling (36" base + 18" splash + 42" uppers = 96"
  exactly), and only ~12" of wall shows above the 80" patio-door head. Both are 8'-plate signatures;
  at 9' you'd see an ~12" cabinet reveal and ~2' above door heads.

**Verdict: 8'0" typical plates on all three floors, confirmed to ±2" by measurement — the OM table is
accurate, not a typo.** The design compensates with glazing: window heads are set high (~7'4") with
stacked/corner window walls, which is why units photograph brighter and taller than an 8' plate suggests.
Remaining items: laser-confirm on the tour (formality), get the penthouse unit schedule (which
buildings/stacks carry the 15–16' vaults), and confirm no floor-3 vault conditions elsewhere.

## 8. Cost context for the construction team

- Broker-observed hard bids on two comparable in-process Boise garden deals: **$305k and $315k/unit**, and
  "Seasons arguably a little more" — replacement "north of $315k" (Naumann, 8/6).
- Our own bottoms-up work: **~$322.5k/unit (~$116.1M)** (`research/audit_2026-08/replacement_cost_analysis.md` v3).
- TMG ES 08.17: recommended bid $122.0M ($338,889/unit) vs guidance $125M ($347,222/unit) — i.e., basis
  ~5–7% above replacement cost; the construction team's read on true like-kind replication cost
  (given the outsized amenity program, 1.81/unit parking, and 13-building spread) sharpens that spread.
- Note the amenity program is land-hungry (Naumann: "Seasons had the space and they took advantage of it…
  much more like the Prelude deal") — replicating it requires ~16 AC at ~23 du/ac, which no entitled
  competitor in the ring currently has.
