**Subject: Seasons at Meridian — post-IC updates, broker guidance, submitting at $119M**

Team —

Quick update on Seasons since Monday. We've tightened the underwriting in five places, got real
guidance from the broker yesterday, and we intend to submit at $119M. Detail below but the punchline:
the book got better where it counts (real quotes and third-party support replacing assumptions), the
returns moved modestly against us at the same price, and the bid basis now more than covers it.

**Where the model sits.** Monday's deck was 8.7% UIRR / 11.9% LIRR at $122M. Same $122M through the
current model is 8.6% / 11.3%. At the $119M we plan to submit, back of the envelope is **~9.3% UIRR /
~13% LIRR and a 5.07% Y1 cap** (re-cutting the model at $119 now — will move a little with final debt
sizing, if anything slightly in our favor since the tax reassessment scales with price). Basis at $119M
is ~$331K/unit, still ~2.5% ABOVE our $322.5K/unit replacement cost work — we're not underwriting a
discount-to-replacement story that doesn't exist, and at this basis we don't need to.

**What changed since Monday (net at $122M: Y1 NOI +$139K, Y1 cap 4.76% → 4.87%; UIRR −19bps, LIRR
−61bps):**

1. **Taxes — KEAndrews engaged (Clayton House).** They project the 2027 reassessment at $120.6M
   (96.5% of guidance) → $533K of 2027 taxes vs $419K in place, with the levy rate compressing ~2%/yr.
   Functionally where we already were — Year 1 taxes actually came down ~$6K — but the sale-triggered
   step-up is now third-party supported rather than our own estimate, which matters for B&F and for the
   lender. Note KEA anchored to the $125M guidance; at $119M their method implies ~$26K/yr less than
   we're carrying. Conservative as is.

2. **Insurance — Karen's feedback: $525/u → $500/u.** Small ($9K in Y1) but real, and still ~1.5x
   what the seller actually carries, so nobody can call it thin.

3. **Debt — CBRE soft quotes in hand (Jay Wagley, PLA dated 8/18).** We're now modeling an actual
   quote instead of a placeholder: Freddie 5-yr fixed, max buydown — **5.31% all-in** (140 over the
   5-yr, bought down 46bps), **$73.3M / ~59% LTV, full-term IO**, defeasance. That's cheaper than the
   5.40% we carried but smaller than the $76.7M placeholder — equity at close goes up ~$3.3M, and
   candidly that's most of the levered give-back since Monday. The grid also gives us optionality we
   didn't have Monday: Fannie at 5.37%, a floater at 5.15%, and a forward starting month 24 at 4.91%
   if we want to think about it. All sized at 1.25x — nothing here is stretched.

4. **Y1 market rent — seasonality re-cut with asset management: $1,915 → $1,885 (−1.6%).** This is
   the Prelude lesson applied — we ran ~2.5% light the first two quarters there because we drew the
   recovery starting the quarter we closed. Y1 now equals today's L5 executed leases ($1,885), i.e.
   zero recovery assumed in the Y1 average, with T90 market already at $1,940. Because growth rates
   are unchanged the cut carries through the whole path, which is what costs us the exit (−$1.7M gross)
   and most of the UIRR. I'd rather give those bps back than re-learn that lesson with IC watching.

5. **CapEx — Justin rebuilt the budget bottoms-up: −$485K.** Line-item schedule replaces the $4,750/u
   allowance we carried into Monday (and that CBRE has in the PLA) — now $1.23M all-in over the hold
   ($3,404/u incl. reserves), front-half weighted.

**What the new T12 + rent roll told us (July T12, 8/18 RR).** The lease-up is finished —
**352 of 360 occupied (97.8%)**, 8 vacants, concessions in July were $3.8K against a $35K/mo T12

buying**, and it's converting: 64 move-ins since June 1 at an average $1,920, the 17 August move-ins
at $1,947 — both above our new $1,885 Y1 number. T3 NOI ticked up vs the June statement. Short
version: the asset is arriving at our underwriting before we've closed.
