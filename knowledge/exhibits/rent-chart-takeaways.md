## SLIDE 3 — box copy

- **What you're looking at.** Solid lines are *actual* trailing-90-day, mix-weighted market rents built
  from lease-level HelloData; dashed lines are *our underwritten* market rents; diamonds are the last-5
  executed new leases off each rent roll. Seasons in blue, Prelude in orange.
- **Both assets have round-tripped a full cycle and sit at new highs.** Seasons bottomed at $1,677
  (Dec-25) and is **$1,939 today — +15.7% off the trough, +7.2% YoY**. Prelude bottomed at $1,629 (Mar-26)
  and is **$1,793 — +10.0% off the trough, +3.9% YoY**. The 2025 trough is seasonal softness plus the tail
  of Seasons' lease-up, not a rent-level reset.
- **The underwriting hands off *below* today's market.** The Seasons dashed line starts at the last actual
  ($1,939, 8/13/26); the **Y1 average of $1,915 (4Q26–3Q27) is 1.3% below today's spot** and only 1.6%
  above the $1,885 L5. Y1 asks for no new asking growth — only that we hold what is already signed.
- **Why the diamonds sit below the lines.** L5 takes the five most recent leases *per floor plan*, so on
  slow-turning plans it reaches well past 90 days — S1, B1 and both C1s (**75 of 360 units**) reach back
  **155–180 days**, into 1Q-26 pricing. Holding the window constant, that reach is only ~$15 of the $55
  gap; the other ~$40 is advertised asking vs. signed contract rent. **The two marks bracket the $1,915
  Y1 UW rather than contradict it.**
- **Prelude's two diamonds are the live proof in our own portfolio.** Executed new leases went **$1,718 at
  closing (12/3/25) → $1,811 today, +5.4% in eight months** — already 2.0% above the model's 2027 market
  rent, with the current T90 running 2.7% ahead of the Q3-26 UW point.
- **The watch item is the winter.** The UW carries Seasons down only **4.3%** from today's peak to 1Q27
  ($1,855); each of the last two winters ran **−6% to −7%** (Aug → 1Q), which off today's level implies
  **$1,800–$1,820**.

## Supporting detail

**1. The two recoveries are mechanically different.** Seasons' is concession-led — effective rent went
$1,420 (Nov-25) → $1,939 today, **+36.6%**, as concessions burned off: **zero of the last 49 leases
carried a concession, vs 67 of 67 a year ago.** Asking now equals effective. Prelude's is level-led: its
asking/effective spread has been ≤2.5% for most of 12 months, so its +10.0% is real rent, not discount
burn-off. Worth keeping straight, since the Seasons Y1 bridge leans on the concession lever specifically.

**2. Anchors as of 8/13/26.**

| Anchor | Seasons | Prelude |
|---|---|---|
| T90 asking | $1,939 | $1,793 |
| T90 effective | $1,939 | $1,774 |
| L5 executed new lease | $1,885 (8/4/26 RR) | $1,811 (8/13/26 RR) |
| Avg new lease, May–Jul '26 | $1,881 | $1,780 |
| New-lease trade-out, May–Jul '26 | +6.6% | +6.5% |
| UW Y1 market-rent average | **$1,915** (4Q26–3Q27) | **$1,730** (CY 2026) |

**3. Decomposing the $54.68 Seasons T90-vs-L5 gap.** Two effects, and the stale-lease reach is the
smaller one:

| Step | Mix-wtd rent | Delta |
|---|---|---|
| T90 asking, HelloData, 90-day window | $1,939 | — |
| Last-5 per plan, **same** HelloData asking source | $1,924 | **−$15** — window/date reach |
| Model L5, rent-roll **executed contract rent** | $1,885 | **−$39** — asking vs. signed |

Days back to find five leases by plan: B2 and B3a 31, A1 47, A2 54, B3b 103 — but **C1a/C1b 155 days
(3/11/26), S1 174 (2/20/26), B1 180 (2/14/26)**. Those 75 units carry rents set when the property's T90
was ~$1,700, ~12% below today; as they turn, the L5 marker catches up to the T90 line with no market
movement at all. The residual ~$39 (−2.0%) is definitional — HelloData's *last asking rent* is the
advertised list price, the rent roll's L5 is signed contract rent.

**It does not always run this direction.** At Prelude the same decomposition inverts: T90 $1,793 → HD
last-5 $1,809 (the fast-turning 2BRs reach back only 23–63 days, so in a rising market the last five sit
*above* the 90-day average) → executed L5 $1,811, i.e. essentially **no** asking-to-signed spread. Read
the sign of the T90-vs-L5 gap as information about turnover speed and pricing discipline at each
property, not as a rule.

**4. Prelude entered 2026 behind its UW and is exiting ahead.** CY-26 asking averaged $1,692 Jan–Jul vs
the $1,730 UW year-average (−2.2%) because the model didn't underwrite the Feb–Mar dip. Current spot is
2.7% *above* the Q3-26 UW point, and it needs ~$1,783 Aug–Dec to make the CY-26 average — it is at $1,793
today. Shape difference, not level miss. (In-place rent separately tracks +0.7% ahead of the $1,668 UW
rent-per-occupied-unit path.)

**5. Read-through to the Seasons Y1 revenue bridge.** Per the deal knowledge base the Y1 bet is
(a) averaging today's executed asking across Nov-26–Oct-27, (b) concessions burning to ~1.5–3.0% of AGPR,
and (c) 94.5% occupancy. These slides directly de-risk (a) and (b) — asking is above the Y1 average
today, concessions are at zero on new signings, and trade-outs are printing 6.6%. They say nothing about
(c), which is the gap the turnover counts flag.

## Caveats to keep visible

- T90 is a **trailing-90-day** window, so the August points are July-weighted peak-season signings; not a
  full-year run rate.
- UW lines are **period averages** plotted against monthly spot actuals. Do not read a single crossing
  point as "ahead" or "behind."
- Sample sizes in a 90-day window are 36–67 leases per property — enough to trend, thin enough that any
  single month can move $20–30.
- Seasons' pre-2026 asking is lease-up pricing from a 2024 delivery; comparisons to that period measure a
  lease-up-to-stabilized transition, not pure market rent growth.
- The §3 decomposition is an **approximation**: the model's L5 keys off rent-roll *move-in* date while the
  HelloData last-5 keys off *off-market* date, so the two "last five" sets are near-identical cohorts but
  not the same leases.
- Slide 4's turnover block filters new leases for term variance >4 months; move-out counts appear
  unfiltered, so the 49-vs-52 / 23-vs-31 comparison is indicative only.
