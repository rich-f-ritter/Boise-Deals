## Headline findings

1. **Union 93 is dead — but its skeleton just came back to life as "Heritage Square."** The property was sold at an Ada County Sheriff's foreclosure auction in Oct 2025 to Tommy Ahlquist (Gray Owl Holdings) + The Pacific Companies, and on **July 13, 2026** (three days ago) they announced Heritage Square: the existing concrete skeleton repurposed into two residential towers totaling **~250 units** + 120k sf office + 32k sf retail, targeting a late-2026/early-2027 start. The SaM chart row should be renamed, cut 350→250, and restored from Bear-only to live pipeline (~Q1 2029).
2. **The Cole Denton quietly died and was reborn bigger.** The $11.2M foundation/podium permit was actually ISSUED 4/5/2024 — then expired 3/31/2026 unbuilt. In May/June 2026 a new filing (PLN26-00493) appeared on the same parcel: a **six-story, 200+-unit** mixed-use building, now at the neighborhood-meeting stage. Re-date 136→~200u, delivery 2029+.
3. **Kootenai & Federal Way (Hawkins) was upsized.** The Boise Design Review Commission unanimously approved the redesigned scheme on **Dec 10, 2025**: six stories, **200 units** (was 5-story/194), 319 podium stalls. Still no building permit through June 2026 → stays PROPOSED, ~Q1 2029.
4. **Two SaM rows are dead paper and should be dropped:** Cobalt Point (264u — CUP withdrawn by the applicant Dec 1, 2022, marketing domain dead) and Fairview/Biltmore (150u — CUP withdrawn June 2023; the site has been an operating RV dealership since April 2024).
5. **The "entitled-but-for-sale" pattern is endemic.** Ascent Overland (CBRE, $5.0M ask), The Ranahan (listing now pulled), 12548 W Overland (listed the week it was entitled; PUD likely lapsed ~Mar 2026), and Meridian OZ (downsized 60→36u in Oct 2024, then listed at $1.7M, went pending, now off-market) are all sponsors selling entitlements, not building. None supports a delivery date.
6. **The two nearest-term real threats remain The Judy and Roundhouse's Victory.** The Judy's OM shows permits estimated to issue ~July 21, 2026 (re-check in Aug–Sep — likely promotion to UC). Roundhouse filed building permits for the 300-unit Victory in early June 2026 targeting a fall-2026 groundbreaking (re-check Oct–Nov).
7. **Base-case vs Bear-case split shifts materially.** Of CR's 1,982 proposed units, only ~500 (Victory 300 + Kootenai/Federal 200) show live forward motion; the other ~1,488 (Vista Point 892, Londoner 189, 3300 S Vista 183, Ranahan 138, Station Village 86) are stalled/dead and belong in Bear-only. Of SaM's 3,646, the live set is ~1,082 (Heritage Square 250, Pine 43 270, Rolling Hill 200, Cole Denton ~200, The Judy 162); ~2,090 is stalled Bear-only; 414 (Cobalt + Fairview) should be dropped.
