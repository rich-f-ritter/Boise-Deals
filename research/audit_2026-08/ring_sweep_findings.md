# 5-Mile Ring Re-Audit — Missed / Changed Supply (Aug 14, 2026)

Systematic sweep for multifamily projects invisible to the CoStar/RealPage-based roster,
triggered by the Overland & Wells II (Seasons at Meridian II) miss. Sources cited inline;
BoiseDev/Statesman/KIVI/IBR + Meridian agendas.

## New finds (to add to Supply Chart)

### 1. Pine 43 vertically-integrated residential — up to 604 additional units (SHADOW)
H-2024-0071 (approved 10/21/2025, 4-1) is already tracked for its 270-unit conventional CUP
("Pine 43 Multifamily (Pine & Webb)"), but the same MDA also allows **up to 604 vertically
integrated units** over ground-floor commercial (87 ft height exception) plus 30 townhomes —
up to 904 total new units in the Pine 43 master plan (~1.8 mi). The VI increment is in
nobody's pipeline count. Filed as a modification to a 15-year-old master plan under the
legacy name — the same trap as Overland & Wells II.
Sources: boisedev.com 2025/09/22 + 2025/10/22; Meridian P&Z agenda 9/18/25.

### 2. Outer Banks Apartments — 516 units, groundbreaking expected 2026 (PROPOSED→near-UC)
Elk Ventures; CUP 2024: 516 units (364 apts, 126 flats, 26 TH) on 19.34 ac R-40/C-C at
SW corner Franklin & Ten Mile (~3.3 mi). Statesman 2026 outlook: groundbreaking expected
2026, 14–16-month build, first units summer 2027. Was an unquantified watch-list row
("Outer Banks Subdivision") — promote to a numbered 516-unit pipeline row.

### 3. The District at Ten Mile — ~1,800-unit master plan, broke ground May 2026 (SHADOW)
Ball Ventures Ahlquist + Fred Bruning, 222 ac NW quadrant I-84 & Ten Mile (~3.3 mi).
Retail-led (Target, Life Time, hotels) but ~1,800 residential units incl. apartments and
up to 400 townhomes (vertical from ~June 2026). Rental MF phases unbranded/uncounted.
Sources: KIVI; IBR 6/22/26; ahlquistdev.com.

### 4. Modern Craftsman Franklin — 122 BTR units, Franklin & Black Cat (~4.0 mi) (WATCH)
Baron Properties BTR (56×1BR, 46×2BR, 20×3BR; SFR/duplex/6-plex), annexation+CUP+plat
filed 2023. Post-2023 status unverified — name collides with built Modern Craftsman Ten
Mile. Verify with Meridian planning before counting.
Source: boisedev.com 2023/02/17.

## Takeaway
The same three blind spots that hid Overland & Wells II — legacy-name master-plan
modifications, plat-style names with no apartment brand, and quiet approval swaps on
parcels with a dead prior deal — account for ~1,900+ un-rostered or mis-counted units
inside the ring (Pine 43 VI + Outer Banks + District at Ten Mile MF phases + Cole Denton
delta).
