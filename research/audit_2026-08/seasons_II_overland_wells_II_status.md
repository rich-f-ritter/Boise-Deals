# Overland & Wells II / "Seasons at Meridian II" — Definitive Status (Aug 14, 2026)

**VERDICT: DEAD — DENIED, never entitled. Not competitive supply; keep as documented
shadow-watch entry with ~5% de-novo revival risk (earliest plausible delivery ~2030).**

## The record
- **H-2022-0030** (MDA to existing DA Inst. #2016-060157 + CUP), applicant Morgan Stonehill
  Partners (agent; owner of record **WinCo Foods, LLC**), 2600 E Overland Rd. 351 units
  marketed; **345 units** at hearing after the vertically-integrated component was carved out.
- **P&Z recommended denial 3-1** (Aug 4, 2022) — "traffic concerns and that the original
  approval was retail space." Staff had recommended approval; council sided with P&Z.
- **City Council DENIED both requests** at the Oct 25, 2022 hearing (continued from Sept 6);
  **Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law for Denial adopted Nov 9, 2022** (consent agenda).
  Denial rationale: loss of employment/job-creating land; failing roadway segments and
  turning movements at Overland/Eagle; over-concentration of multifamily next to the
  360-unit Phase 1; applicant failed to show the modification was "superior to the existing
  Development Agreement." Meridian noted an MDA denial is not subject to judicial review
  (Brown v. City of Meridian); no reconsideration/appeal found.
- **No CUP ever existed → no commencement clock, no TEC/TECC extension possible.** No
  re-application 2023–2026.

## The land today
- Still owned by **WinCo Foods, LLC** (PO Box 5756, Boise) — Morgan Stonehill never took
  title; Phase 1's sale (Carlyle capital) did not include this parcel.
- **Feb 5, 2025**: WinCo requested a lot split (~12 ac north / ~6 ac south along the planned
  Cinema Drive extension); "no specific plans for development on either" (Idaho Statesman,
  Feb 27, 2025). Final Plat "Winco Wells Subdivision No. 2" (FP-2018-0018) on Feb 18, 2025

  S1117438630 — renumbering in the WinCo Wells platting sequence.)
- Architect NG+P's "Concept Design/Entitlements" page and Mar 2023 Multifamily Dive copy are
  **stale pre-denial marketing** — rejected as activity signals on the recency lens.

## Underwriting read-through
The denial is actually a **positive datapoint for the subject**: the council's stated reason
included MF over-concentration at this node — a precedent that protects the subject's
immediate submarket from the most direct possible competitor. The residual risks on this
parcel are retail development (WinCo big-box + pads: amenity-positive, traffic-negative) and
a future political shift enabling a de novo MF application.
