## Pattern read (what the graveyard says about the submarket)
- **Meridian council kills big MF at retail/employment nodes** (Overland & Wells II,
  Fairview, Cobalt Point withdrawals under pressure) — protective for the subject's node.
- **Denial is often round one, not the end**: Rolling Hill, Centrepoint, Union 93 and
  Tanner Creek all came back in new form. A dead file on good dirt = future pipeline.
- **The Magic View enclave has killed senior/MF three times** (2019, 2021 → now for-sale
  SF); the closest-in land to the subject keeps failing to produce rentals.
- 2024-2025 was a cull: ~2,100 units died in-ring in 24 months (Bridge 549, The 10 559,
  Newkirk 216, Madison 251, Hummingbird 170, Centrepoint-affordable 239, LH&FM 118).
