# The District at Ten Mile — Underwriting Dossier
*~220-acre master-planned development, NW corner of S. Ten Mile Rd & I-84, Meridian, ID. Broke ground May 5, 2026. Researched Aug 13, 2026 for underwriting of Seasons at Meridian (2700 E Overland Rd, ~2.5 mi east / 4 mi by road). Every item flagged CONFIRMED / REPORTED (single-source press) / RUMOR-UNVERIFIED.*

> **Correction to earlier repo research:** the master developer is **Ahlquist Development** (with Ball Ventures and Elevated Property Company) — NOT Hawkins Companies or Kuna-based developers. Hawkins is active at a *different* node (Meridian Rd/I-84: Syringa Crossing 408 units; the unconfirmed ~150K SF warehouse-retail concept). Prior attribution was conflation.

---

## 1. Sponsorship & ownership

- **Master developer: Ahlquist Development, Meridian (CEO Tommy Ahlquist).** Launched Jan 2, 2024 as a three-party venture: **Ball Ventures** (Idaho Falls, Ball family) + **Ball Ventures Ahlquist (BVA)** + **Elevated Property Company (EPC)** — EPC formed by **Fred Bruning**, founder/ex-CEO of CenterCal (developer of The Village at Meridian), with ex-CenterCal executives Sean Dennison and Chris Byers. Project billed at **$1B+ total cost**. BVA rebranded to Ahlquist Development July 1, 2024; Ball relationship continues. [CONFIRMED]
- Track record: Ten Mile Crossing (with Brighton, across the street), Eagle View Landing, Pleasant Valley industrial, CWI Boise campus.
- **2026 partners:** **Adler Industrial** (industrial/flex, west end — explicitly separate development per city staff), **McAlvain Companies** (GC), **Raymond Management Company** (both hotels; WI-based). Leasing: Mark Cleverley (CLO), Holt Haga. [CONFIRMED]
- **Land:** assembled farmland under 2009/2011 annexation development agreements; underlying owner entities in city files: Fedrizzi Ten Mile LLC, SJJV LLC, Janicek Properties LLC (+ BVA's Vanguard Village DA). **Sale prices not public** (Idaho non-disclosure); Ahlquist said April 2026 the venture had **closed on the first 100 acres**. [CONFIRMED / REPORTED]

## 3. Residential — the competitive-supply read (most important)

**Headline "~1,800 units" is a full-buildout marketing number spanning ~5–10 years. No apartment developer, builder, or named project is attached to any District parcel in any public source through Aug 2026.** Beneath the number:

- **Vanguard Village — 552 apartment units entitled** at 1085 S. Ten Mile Rd (SWC Franklin/Ten Mile, the carved-out residential piece; R-40; H-2021-0081/H-2023-0072; final plat in process 2024). **No vertical start reported as of mid-2026.** Plausible delivery 2027–2029. This is the nearest-term real risk from the site.
- **TN-C northwest corridor (~33 ac): 268–402 units of townhome + MF** entitled at 8–12 du/ac — the press's "up to 400 townhomes," **Phase 3 (2028+)**.
- Jan 2024 launch materials: 72 ac residential, "at least 1,000 apartments" — the balance beyond the above is **paper units today**.
- Developer rhetoric (council transcript): denser, "**more affordable product**," units "coming online ~2028" — i.e., if built, likely positioned below the subject, competing on price not product.
- **Corridor context (the real number that matters):** ~3,000 MF units approved in the Ten Mile planning area, ~1,900 built (mid-2024); Gateway at Ten Mile 390 (approved Nov 2024); Outer Banks 516; **Southridge 640 u/c on Overland south of I-84 — the closest of all of these to the subject**; plus District paper units.

**Early-warning indicator: Meridian building permits / CZC filings for "Vanguard Village No. 1."** That is the tripwire for the District's apartments actually starting.

## 4. Phasing & timeline

- 2024–spring 2025: mass grading (173K CY), W. Grand Mogul Dr spine road (Ten Mile↔Black Cat) built; two Adler flex buildings u/c late 2025.
- **May 5, 2026: groundbreaking. Phase 1 opens spring 2027** (Target May 2027, Life Time, pads, daycare, flex; hotels underway).
- Phase 2: balance of 650K SF; developer says Phases 1–2 complete in ~3–4 years (≈2029–30). Phase 3 (lifestyle center, townhomes, 4 more flex buildings): 2028+, explicitly demand-dependent.
- Infrastructure: Grand Mogul spine, Cobalt Dr, "Umbria" collector to Franklin, private streets; Williams gas pipeline crosses site. **Planned grade-separated "Vanguard connection" under I-84 linking Ten Mile Crossing to the District (per ITD plan, cited in council transcript) — timing/funding unpublished.**

## 5. Entitlements, public finance, opposition

- **TIF: almost certainly NOT in the Ten Mile URD** (the 2016, 301-ac district lies north of I-84 and east of Ten Mile — the Brighton side). No TIF/reimbursement agreement found. High-confidence inference — verify at title/OS if it matters.
- Entitlement chain: annexations 2009 (AZ-09-008) & 2011 (AZ-11-001); Vanguard Village H-2021-0081 (R-40/M-E/H-E/C-C/TN-C) & H-2023-0072; **District H-2023-0071** (CPAM + consolidated DA + ~57-ac rezone + 108.77-ac preliminary plat): P&Z unanimous Aug 15, 2024 **despite staff recommending denial** (insufficient use-mixing, FARs far below the Ten Mile Interchange Specific Area Plan); Council continued Sept 17 → approved (reported Oct 1, 2024). Developer got its own design-standards regime in the DA (mirroring Brighton's precedent).
- Opposition: 13 letters (traffic, schools, parks, "no more high-density housing"); **applicant declined to file a phasing plan**; ACHD stated **no further capacity improvements are contemplated for Ten Mile Rd** (buildout condition).
- **In-N-Out:** denied at Ten Mile/Lost Rapids (P&Z 4-1 Apr 2025; council appeal rejected 5-1 Sept 2025); pivoted to the District (Mar 2026); **drive-thru CUP at the District not yet approved as of Aug 13, 2026** — modest tenant risk.

## 6. Traffic/access

- Ten Mile SPUI interchange (2011) congested; no near-term capacity fix — ITD's I-84 EB aux-lane package is programmed **FY2031** and folded into the Garrity-to-Wye Mobility Improvement Study.
- **SH-16 freeway extension complete 2027** — the biggest access change, feeding the District's west flank (and pulling the valley's growth axis west).
- ACHD nearby: Ustick widening (2025–26), **Linder Rd I-84 overpass (construction 2026–27** — a parallel crossing 1 mi east of Ten Mile), Franklin widening (TBD).

## 7. Market meaning for the subject

- Positioned as "the geographic center of the Treasure Valley" — The Village at Meridian at ~2.5× the footprint ("Village 2.0"), aimed at capturing west-valley spend that today drives to Eagle Rd.
- **Demand-positive** for the corridor (Target/Life Time/In-N-Out as leasing amenities for every I-84 apartment community, subject included) and **employment-positive** at the margin (Life Time 200+, Target ~150–250 typical, hotels, ~500K SF industrial) — but **no aggregate jobs figure exists**; the retail/hospitality jobs skew modest-wage.
- **Supply-negative with a lag:** its own apartments are 2028+ paper (except Vanguard 552 entitled); the corridor's built pipeline (Southridge 640 closest) is the nearer pressure.
- **Competitive frame vs. the subject's node:** the District strengthens Ten Mile as the west anchor, but it does not add a single unit or job *at* Eagle/I-84 — consistent with the two-node thesis in the location-premium memo: employment mass + supply scarcity east (subject), growth + supply abundance west.

## 8. Kill/delay risks

- Momentum real: land closed (first 100 ac), grading done, spine road paved, flex u/c, Target/Life Time/hotels underway, 13+ tenants, GC mobilized. Phase 1 spring-2027 credible.
- No capital stack ever disclosed; **Feb 19, 2026: CBRE listed five Ball/Ahlquist joint assets for sale** (Topgolf — relisted at $25M vs $27.5M in 2024 —, Kiln, AmeriBen/Rackham office, Pioneer Crossing) — plausibly capital recycling; watch for sponsor-liquidity read-through.
- Open items: In-N-Out CUP; no phasing plan on file (developer-controlled timing); Phase 3 demand-dependent; theater dropped; office unlet; no interchange relief funded before FY2031; **the ~1,400 non-townhome residential units have no named builders — paper units.**
