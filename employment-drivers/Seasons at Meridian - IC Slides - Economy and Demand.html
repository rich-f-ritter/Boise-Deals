The Qualification Math — How $27–94k Jobs Pay $1.7–1.9k Rents
3.0x screen needs $62k (contract $1,714) to $68k (market $1,897). Construction ($94k) and healthcare ($67k) qualify solo; government/logistics/retail/food ($27–60k) arrive as dual-earner & roommate households — two $50k paychecks = a $100k household at 4.9x. That mechanism converts a $58k median personal income into an $80–89k median household — and 71% of initial leases carried ~1.2 months free now burning off at +9.5–10.7% effective on turn.
