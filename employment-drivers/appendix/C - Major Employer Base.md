# Appendix C — Boise MSA (Treasure Valley) Major-Employer Base
*Research stream 3 of 4. As of August 13, 2026. Boise City MSA (Ada, Canyon, Gem, Boise, Owyhee counties). Total nonfarm employment: ~420,100 (June 2026, BLS), up ~1.2% YoY; metro unemployment ~3.6–3.7%. Labor force ~457,500 (BVEP, 2025).*

**Data caveat up front:** There is no single current official local-headcount census. The best official anchor is the Idaho Dept. of Labor "Top Private Employers >500" list (statewide ranges, **2023 vintage** — stale) plus BLS CES industry data (June 2026). Company-specific figures below are a mix of 2023–2026 vintages; each is year-flagged. Statewide figures for St. Luke's, Albertsons, Simplot, etc. overstate metro-only headcount somewhat; Treasure Valley shares are estimated where flagged.

## 2. Per-employer notes (biggest 10–15)

**Micron (brief, for scale).** ~6,000+ Treasure Valley direct employees (company, Dec 2023) out of ~420k MSA jobs — i.e., **only ~1.5–1.7% of metro employment directly**, though its indirect multiplier is large. Record FY2026 results on AI memory demand. Boise expansion: ID1 fab on track for **first wafer output mid-CY2027**, ID2 late CY2028; ~$50B+ committed locally within a stated $200B program, ~2,000 direct Micron jobs and 15,000+ indirect by ~2030. One wobble: Micron "unexpectedly permanently discontinued" GC Exyte's contract effective Sept 2025 (201 construction-management layoffs), but the project continues under new management. ([Micron Q3 FY26 remarks](https://investors.micron.com/static-files/631b1a32-5537-46ae-8f40-82e42fc79dfe), [IBR 6/16/25](https://idahobusinessreview.com/2025/06/16/micron-boise-chip-plant-expansion/), [Yahoo/Statesman on Exyte](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/micron-dropped-general-contractor-building-110000454.html), [KIVI explainer](https://www.kivitv.com/southeast-boise/everything-you-need-to-know-about-microns-massive-boise-manufacturing-expansion))

**St. Luke's Health System — largest employer in Idaho, growing.** ~16,000+ statewide (IDOL 2023 top-private list, rank #1); ~17,000 per 2025–26 reporting, ~3,600 at the downtown Boise campus alone. Multi-phase downtown Boise expansion (new tower + medical office plaza) continuing **through 2029** despite cost pressure. Nonprofit, so it tops "largest private employer" lists even though Micron is the largest *for-profit*. ([IDOL 2023 list PDF](https://lmi.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2023-Top-Private-Employers-greater-than-500-1.pdf), [IBR 2/3/25](https://idahobusinessreview.com/2025/02/03/st-lukes-downtown-boise-project-continues-despite-rising-costs-and-challenges/), [ENR 4/2026](https://digital.bnpmedia.com/publication/?i=864040&article_id=5146012&view=articleBrowser))

**Albertsons — the key HQ risk to watch.** Boise HQ (250 E Parkcenter Blvd). After courts blocked the Kroger merger (Dec 2024), Albertsons cut corporate/division support staff in **Jan 2025** — company stayed under Idaho WARN thresholds (<50 per filing) but trade press reported several hundred Boise roles affected plus offshoring; $1.5B three-year cost-cut program. **July 2026:** after a same-store-sales miss, Albertsons announced consolidation of 11 regional divisions into 4; the Mountain West division office building next to the Boise HQ is now listed for lease, and the company **declined to answer BoiseDev's questions about Boise impact**. No announced HQ relocation, but corporate Boise headcount is clearly drifting down; underwrite the HQ as a slow-shrink, not a growth engine. ([BoiseDev 1/14/25](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/01/14/albertsons-layoffs/), [BoiseDev 1/21/25](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/01/21/albertsons-2025-layoffs/), [BoiseDev 7/23/26](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/07/23/with-big-sales-drop-albertsons-to-reorganize-divisions-it-wont-say-what-boise-impact-will-be/), [Food Trade News](https://foodtradenews.com/2025/01/20/aftermath-of-a-failed-deal-albertsons-begins-layoffs-in-boise-at-divisions/))

**HP Inc. — confirmed full Boise exit.** The single worst employer headline of 2026: on **March 5, 2026** HP told employees it will **exit its Boise campus by end of 2027**, ending 50+ years in Boise (LaserJet's birthplace), consolidating into Vancouver, WA and Corvallis, OR hubs. Site had ~1,200–1,700 employees (down from ~4,000 at peak decades ago). Mostly higher-income engineering/management jobs — a renter-by-choice and move-up-buyer demand loss for W. Boise/Meridian submarkets, phased over 2026–27. ([BoiseDev 3/5/26](https://boisedev.com/news/2026/03/05/hp-says-it-will-exit-boise-site/), [Boise State Public Radio](https://www.boisestatepublicradio.org/news/2026-03-05/hewlett-packard-to-pull-out-of-boise-in-2027), [East Idaho News](https://www.eastidahonews.com/2026/03/hewlett-packard-announces-it-will-leave-idaho-by-the-end-of-2027/))

**Saint Alphonsus (Trinity Health).** 6,000+ colleagues + 500 medical staff across Idaho/E. Oregon; four medical centers (Boise, Nampa, Ontario, Baker City). No 2024–26 mass layoffs found; Forbes "Best Large Employers 2025"; investing in workforce pipeline (student loan-repayment recruitment program, May 2025). Stable #2 healthcare anchor. ([saintalphonsus.org](https://www.saintalphonsus.org/press-releases/saint-alphonsus-named-one-best-large-employers-america))

**Boise State University.** 3,708 employees (Fall 2025) and **record 28,519 enrollment (+4.7% YoY; largest-ever freshman class +14.7%)** — counter-cyclical demand anchor and pipeline for local employers, though state appropriation cuts pressure staffing. ([BSU news 10/30/25](https://www.boisestate.edu/news/2025/10/30/university-sets-enrollment-record-with-fall-2025-students/), [BSU facts](https://www.boisestate.edu/about/facts/), [IBR 11/7/25](https://idahobusinessreview.com/2025/11/07/idaho-college-enrollment-funding-cuts/))

**Simplot.** HQ in downtown Boise (opened 2016); 3,000+ Idaho employees (IDOL 2023), 15,000–18,000 global. **Sept 2025:** reorganized into "Simplot Company," a global holding structure aligning food, ag-inputs, and international units — centralization read as reinforcing, not diminishing, the Boise HQ. Privately held, multi-generational; stable-to-growing. ([Capital Press 9/15/25](https://capitalpress.com/2025/09/15/simplot-changes-company-name-structure/), [simplot.com](https://www.simplot.com/company/news/simplot-strengthens-centralized-support-and-strategic-oversight))

**WinCo Foods.** Boise HQ (ESOP, ~20,000 companywide, ~140–145 stores in 10 states, ~$10.8B revenue). Actively expanding — AZ, OR, WA openings in 2025, first Colorado filings, new Treasure Valley stores planned (Meridian Rd., Caldwell). HQ + local stores + distribution growing modestly. ([Supermarket News](https://www.supermarketnews.com/new-stores/winco-goes-for-the-win), [BoiseDev 4/2/25](https://boisedev.com/news/2025/04/02/you-asked-caldwell-grocery-store-2/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WinCo_Foods))

**ICCU.** Idaho's largest credit union (~$12B+ assets). Statewide employer (~2,170 per LeadIQ 2025; Revelio shows ~1,240 — treat headcount as uncertain); HQ formally in Chubbuck but its western-Idaho hub is the **Eagle View Landing campus in Meridian** ("headquarters for support staff"), plus a 13-story ICCU-branded tower in downtown Boise and arena naming rights. **Scentsy.** Meridian HQ; hired +1,000 in the COVID boom, now unwinding: −116 Meridian (Apr 2025, 11%), −94 Texas (Mar 2025), −87 Meridian + remote (Mar 2026, another 11%). Trajectory clearly down; est. ~700–900 local. Corporate HQ intact but a shrink story. **Idaho Power/IDACORP.** 1,840 employees (Dec 2025), +3.3% YoY, steady multi-year growth on 2.3% customer growth and data-center/industrial load. Quiet, high-wage, stable Boise HQ anchor. ## 3. Eds, meds, and government stability base

Per BLS CES for the Boise MSA (June 2026): **Education & health services 67,300** + **Government 52,700** (fed + state + local incl. public schools) = **120,000 jobs, ~28.6% of the 420,100 nonfarm total**. Add private "quasi-stable" anchors (utilities, groceries HQs) and roughly a third of metro employment is recession-insensitive. Boise's status as state capital means state agencies, the Legislature, courts, and Boise State are disproportionately concentrated in Ada County; healthcare (St. Luke's + Saint Al's ≈ 22,000+ statewide combined, majority in the Valley) is the fastest-growing large block. ([BLS Boise MSA page](https://www.bls.gov/regions/west/id_boisecity_msa.htm))

## 4. Federal/military/airport anchors

- **NIFC:** ~650 year-round employees (up to ~1,000 peak fire season) across 8–9 agencies (BLM ~300, USFS, NWS, etc.) on a 55-acre campus by the airport. National wildfire logistics HQ — sticky, but exposed to federal workforce policy; no confirmed NIFC-specific 2025 RIFs found. ([nifc.gov](https://www.nifc.gov/about-us), [BLM blog 1/13/25](https://www.blm.gov/blog/2025-01-13/inside-look-operations-national-interagency-fire-center))
- **Idaho National Guard / Gowen Field:** ~5,255 employees statewide (cited as Idaho's 4th-largest employer, behind St. Luke's, Walmart, Micron); Air Guard ~1,300 (600+ full-time) at Gowen; ~$650M annual economic impact / ~8,000 supported jobs. Key de-risking event: the Air Force selected Gowen for an **F-16 mission beginning 2027**, replacing the retiring A-10s — securing the base's flying-mission future. ([Gowen Strong](https://www.gowenstrong.com/economic-impact.html), [Idaho Military Division](https://www.imd.idaho.gov/f-16-mission-coming-to-gowen-starting-in-2027/))
- **Boise Airport (BOI):** record passenger traffic (2024–25); **$700M Concourse A/terminal expansion** (Hensel Phelps CM/GC, projects through 2029: +10 gates, rental-car center opening 2026, parking garages). Airport generates **$2.9B annual economic impact, supports 21,000+ jobs**; businesses within the airport master-plan area employ **15,000+** with $510.7M payroll. ([City of Boise 9/2025](https://www.cityofboise.org/news/airport/2025/september/cleared-for-construction-boi-selects-hensel-phelps-for-major-terminal-expansion/), [ENR](https://www.enr.com/articles/61341-hensel-phelps-to-lead-boise-airports-700m-expansion), [iflyboise economic impact](https://www.iflyboise.com/commercial-opportunities/economic-opportunities/economic-impact/))

## 5. Concentration risk synthesis

- **Micron is big but not dominant:** ~6,000–7,000 direct Treasure Valley jobs ≈ **1.5–1.7% of MSA employment** (vs. single-employer towns at 10%+). Even at ~9,000 by 2030 it's ~2% direct. Its real footprint is indirect (construction, suppliers, income effects) — meaningful cyclicality, not existential concentration. Memory is currently in a strong AI-driven upcycle (record FY26), but it is a deeply cyclical business; the 2022–23 downturn produced Boise layoffs.
- **Largest employer overall: St. Luke's** (nonprofit, ~16–17k statewide). **Largest for-profit: Micron** (Idaho-based) with Walmart largest by statewide retail count. No private employer exceeds ~4% of MSA jobs.
- **The 2024–26 pattern is "meds-and-eds up, legacy corporate down":** healthcare, government, education, utility, grocery, logistics, and military anchors are all stable-to-growing, while the shrink list is concentrated in white-collar corporate: **HP (full exit by 2027, ~1,200–1,700 jobs), Albertsons corporate (rolling cuts + division consolidation), Scentsy (−22% in two rounds), Lamb Weston, Blue Cross (−9%), Clearwater (flat locally)**. Net: the metro keeps adding jobs (+1.2% YoY) but is rotating away from tech/HQ office employment toward healthcare, public sector, advanced manufacturing (Micron), and logistics.
- **For multifamily underwriting:** the demand base is well diversified with a ~29% eds/meds/gov ballast plus Micron's committed 2027–2028 fab ramp (including a multi-thousand construction workforce through ~2028). The two headline risks to higher-end renter demand are the HP exit (2027) and any escalation of Albertsons HQ erosion; both are W. Boise/Meridian-skewed. Workforce-housing demand drivers (healthcare, schools, logistics, Guard, airport) look the most secure.

## Staleness flags
- IDOL top-private-employer ranges = **2023** — the most-cited "official" list is 2.5 years old; St. Luke's/Micron have grown since, Albertsons/HP have shrunk.
- WeKnowBoise and similar realtor "top employer" pages recycle ~2021 data (e.g., Blue Cross "900") — superseded above.
- West Ada staff count is an **estimate** (no reliable published figure; ZoomInfo's "694" is wrong for a 38,900-student district).
- ICCU and Clearwater headcounts conflict across data vendors (noted in-line).
- Boise VA (~1,000+ est.) and T-Mobile/Citi Meridian counts are unverified; LinkedIn-derived figures materially undercount.
